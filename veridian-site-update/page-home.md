## 6. "WHAT WE DO"

H2: What we do

INTRO: Seven services across both revenue engines: winning new business, and getting full value from the customers you already have.

Seven cards in this order, each with the link text "How this works →":

1. **Contract Monetisation** (→ /contract-monetisation)
Set the contract base against actual usage, size the gap and recover it. Then put new contracts in place on terms and charging models built for how software is bought now.

2. **Renewals and Retention** (→ /renewals-and-retention)
Design the renewal process: who owns each renewal, when it starts, how price rises are set and what happens when a customer wants to cut. Gross retention first, so the uplift you take is earned.

3. **Outsourced Revenue Operations** (→ /revenue-operations)
Forecasting, deal desk and pipeline governance run as a service, for businesses that need revenue operations but not yet a full-time person or team.

4. **Advisory** (→ /advisory)
Board advisory and fractional CRO support for software companies and the investors behind them. The revenue engine built before you hire someone to run it.

5. **Revenue Turnaround and Recovery** (→ /services/revenue-turnaround-and-recovery)
For businesses behind plan when nobody can say why. Find the cause in weeks, not quarters, and put a plan in front of the board it can track.

6. **Exit Readiness** (→ /exit-readiness)
The revenue operating rhythm buyers pay for, put in place alongside your sales leader 18 to 24 months before a sale, and two years of data that prove it.

7. **Revenue Due Diligence** (→ /revenue-due-diligence)
For investors buying a software business. We rebuild the revenue plan from the company's own data and tell you plainly what it will deliver.
