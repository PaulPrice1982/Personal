## The Revenue Engine Check

**What we collect.** If you complete the Revenue Engine Check at /revenue-engine-check and ask for your full read, we collect your answers to the 18 statements, the ARR band you chose, your result, your name, your work email address and your company name. Nothing is sent until you submit the form. If you only score the statements and leave the page, your answers stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

**Why we use it.** To reply to you about your result, with your full read and an offer of a call. Our lawful basis is your consent, given by ticking the required box, and our legitimate interest in responding to a request you have made.

**Marketing.** We send you occasional articles only if you tick the optional box. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by writing to hello@veridian-partners.com.

**Who sees it.** Veridian Revenue Partners. Your details are sent to us through the same form service as our contact form and are not shared with anyone else or used to build profiles.

**How long we keep it.** If no engagement follows, we delete your details and answers 24 months after your submission. If we go on to work together, they are kept with our client records.

**Your rights.** The rights described elsewhere in this notice apply in full, including access, correction and deletion. Contact hello@veridian-partners.com.
