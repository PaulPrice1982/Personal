## SECTION 3 | THE PROBLEM

HEADING: Revenue operations is the job everyone does a bit of. So nobody does it.

Marketing owns the leads. Sales owns the deal. Customer success owns the renewal. Finance owns the invoice. The handoffs between them are where forecasts drift, discounts creep and contracted uplifts quietly don't happen. In most software businesses under £20m, nobody is accountable for those handoffs.

So the CEO rebuilds the pipeline report on a Sunday night. The CFO and the sales leader argue about the number every month, and neither can prove their version. A renewal comes round without the uplift because nobody spotted the date. None of it appears as a line on the P&L. It appears as a missed quarter.

EY puts revenue leakage at 1 to 5% of EBITDA. For a £20m business running at 25% margin, that's £50k to £250k a year walking out of the door. Before you count the deals lost to a forecast nobody believed.
