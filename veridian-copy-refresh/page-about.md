## THE RECORD, BRIEFLY

Nine years inside a PE-backed UK business software group with 20 operating companies and about £1.6bn of ARR. He joined as an account manager and made President's Club in nine months. In July 2017 he founded the group's commercial contracts function as a team of one; it became the group's main engine for growth from existing customers, booking £142.7m against £123.7m of target over nine consecutive years above plan. As Commercial Director he relaunched the retention programme as TrueSight, which delivered 120% net and 90% gross revenue retention across roughly £375m of engaged ARR. As VP Revenue Excellence he held group accountability for a £240m plan across 20 operating companies and more than 1,000 sellers, ran the group weekly business review and the group deal desk, led 65 people across revenue operations and the GTM technology stack, and was the person sent into operating companies that had fallen behind plan.

Before that: enterprise account management at OneAdvanced across payroll, HR and finance software, and new-business sales at Moorepay, closing 20 to 25 deals a month through bank referral partnerships.
